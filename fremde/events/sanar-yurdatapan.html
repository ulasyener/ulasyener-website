Music & film

In 1971, Yurdatapan co-founded ŞAT Yapım with composer Attila Özdemiroğlu — described as Turkey's first music production company — and contributed to numerous Turkish pop hits through the 1970s. He also composed film scores, winning the Golden Orange Award for Best Film Music in 1975 for Arkadaş.

Exile, 1981–1991

In 1981, after attending a film festival in Lefkoşa (Nicosia) with his then-wife, actress and singer Melike Demirağ, the couple were stripped of their Turkish citizenship by the post-coup military regime. They lived abroad for around twelve years, across several countries, before returning to Turkey in 1991.

Human rights activism

From 1995, prompted by the prosecution of writer Yaşar Kemal, Yurdatapan led the "Initiative Against Thought Crime" (Düşünce Suçuna Karşı Girişim), organizing civil-disobedience campaigns in which writers and publishers republished banned texts under their own names. He was imprisoned for about two months in 2000 over the publication of writings by conscientious objectors. He received the Index on Censorship Award (2002) and the Turkish Journalists' Association Press Freedom Award (2015).
